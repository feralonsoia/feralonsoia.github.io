01 ELEVENLABS · LIVE

NovaMart / Eco

A voice customer-support agent that can reason, protect sensitive actions, and control the storefront.

I designed Eco to handle orders, returns, product questions, address changes, and human escalation. The project combines an ElevenLabs voice agent, a structured workflow, identity checks, knowledge, tools, guardrails, and a web experience that reacts to spoken requests.

  • Built two guardrail variants with a 50/50 traffic split.
  • Found a false identity-verification trigger, fixed the routing condition, and added a passing regression test.
  • Connected voice commands to visual product navigation in the storefront.

How I work

Curious enough to explore. Practical enough to ship.

My path into AI has been hands-on: take the concept from a course, open the tool, build something, break it, understand why, and improve it. That loop has taught me more than simply following instructions.

I enjoy the point where technology meets a real person—whether that means making a voice agent safer, helping a model call the right tool, or turning a complex topic into a lesson someone can use.

01Start with the user, not the model.
02Test the uncomfortable edge cases.
